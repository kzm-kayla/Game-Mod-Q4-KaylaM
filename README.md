=== SCRIMBLY MOD: ACTIONS CAPSULE COMBAT PROTOTYPE ===
Midterm Submission - October 2026

DESCRIPTION:
This mod disables traditional weapon usage and replaces combat mechanics 
with a 10-slot dynamic monster capture and deployment system. Every shooter 
weapon in your arsenal functions as a storage containment unit for caught 
Strogg variant tiers.

HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS:
1. Hold down the [H] key to display the Capsule Arsenal Overlay panel on your HUD.
2. Open the console [~] and type 'cheatCapture' to instantly load your roster 
   with custom tiered specimens (Gladiator, Berserk, Gunner, etc.). 
(make sure you turn on give all/give all weapons cheat in case.)
3. Equip any shooter weapon to select its corresponding container slot.
4. Press the number keys (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, etc.) to throw your "capsule" out, launching that weapon's 
   bound companion straight onto the battlefield to follow you and engage enemies.
5. Press the corresponding weapon's number key again to cleanly retract your active companion back 
   into capsule memory storage.
